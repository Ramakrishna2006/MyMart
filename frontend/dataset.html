📊 MyMart dataset explorer

The store runs on 5 related data sets. They start life as CSV files in the data/ folder, are loaded into the SQLite database on first run, and change live as people shop. Every set below explains what it is, why the project needs it, every column, live statistics and the actual rows.

🔗 How the sets connect

Primary keys (PK) uniquely identify a row. Foreign keys (FK) point to a row in another set.

categories (1) ──< (many) products one category has many products users (1) ──< (many) orders one customer places many orders orders (1) ──< (many) order_items one order contains many lines products (1) ──< (many) order_items one product appears in many orders → order_items is the "bridge" that makes orders ↔ products a many-to-many relationship

♻️ Regenerate or reset the data

  • python data/generate_dataset.py - rebuilds the 5 CSV files (fixed random seed, so the output is always the same).
  • python manage.py reset-db - wipes the database and loads the CSV files again.
  • python manage.py export - saves the live tables (including new orders) to exports/*.csv.

All people, phone numbers, e-mails and brands are synthetic. Prices are realistic for Indian groceries in 2026 but are not real offers.
